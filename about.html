About StudyWithK

We're a group of engineering students who believe quality study materials should be free and accessible to everyone — not locked behind paywalls or scattered across the internet.

Our Story

Why StudyWithK Exists

StudyWithK was born out of a simple frustration — finding good study materials for DDCET and GTU Degree exams is unnecessarily hard. Notes are scattered in WhatsApp groups, Google Drive links break, and Telegram channels are unorganized.

We decided to build a clean, organized, and completely free platform where every student can access handwritten notes, PYQ papers, video lectures, and soft copy references — all neatly organized by subject, chapter, and material type.

The name "StudyWithK" is personal — K is for Krrish, the founder and student behind this initiative, studying Computer Engineering at LDCE, Ahmedabad.

Mission

What We Stand For

Our platform is built on three core principles that guide every decision we make.

Always Free

Every note, PYQ, and video link on StudyWithK is completely free. No subscription, no paywall — ever.

Well Organized

Materials are organized by subject → chapter → type so you can find exactly what you need in seconds.

Community Driven

Built for students, by students. We welcome contributions — share your notes and help others succeed.

Always Updated

We add new materials regularly, especially before exam season. Follow our Telegram channel for updates.
