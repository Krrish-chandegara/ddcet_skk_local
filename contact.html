FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is StudyWithK completely free?

Yes! All materials — notes, PYQs, and video links — are 100% free. No registration or subscription needed.

How often are new notes added?

We add new materials regularly, especially before GTU and DDCET exam seasons. Join our Telegram for alerts.

Can I contribute my own notes?

Absolutely! Fill the contact form with the subject "Contribute My Notes" and we'll review and publish them.

My semester/subject is missing. What do I do?

Request it via the contact form! If enough students request the same material, we prioritize it first.
